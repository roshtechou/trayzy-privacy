Trayzy – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Trayzy: Color Sort Puzzle (“Trayzy”, “the game”), an Android game published by Roshtech OÜ, a company registered in Estonia (“we”, “us”).

In short: the game has no accounts and we do not collect or store any personal information about you on our own servers. The game shows advertisements through Google AdMob, and Google collects some information from your device to do that.

1. Who is responsible

Roshtech OÜ, Estonia, is the controller of personal data processed in connection with the game.
Contact: roshtechou@gmail.com

2. Information the game itself keeps

The game stores the following only on your device. It is never sent to us:

  • the level you have reached;
  • how many boosters you have;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration);
  • whether you have read the “How to play” guide;
  • the date and streak of your last daily reward.

This information is deleted when you clear the game’s data or uninstall the game. The game does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location, and it has no user accounts.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is funded by advertising. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited (for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) or Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, Google’s software in the game may collect and process:

InformationWhy Google uses it
Advertising ID of your device (a resettable identifier)Showing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ads and, with your consent, personalising them
IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) can be derivedDelivering ads relevant to your region, preventing fraud, security
Device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version, screen size, network type)Displaying ads correctly, diagnostics, preventing fraud
How you interact with ads (whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped)Measuring ad performance and paying the publisher

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. We see only aggregated reports, such as how many ads were shown and how much revenue they earned.

Google explains how it uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Your consent and choices

  • Consent in Europe. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your choice the first time you open it, using Google’s consent tool. You can agree to personalised ads, refuse, or manage individual options. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.
  • Changing your mind. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Device settings. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • Playing offline. The game works without an internet connection. No ads are loaded and no information is sent to Google while your device is offline.

5. Legal bases (for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) for storing or reading information on your device for advertising and for personalised ads.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for showing non-personalised ads, measuring them, and preventing fraud, which fund the game.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone other than Google as described above. Google may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States. For transfers from Europe, Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses.

7. How long information is kept

Information stored by the game stays on your device until you clear the game’s data or uninstall it. We keep no personal information ourselves. Google keeps the information it collects for the periods described in its own privacy policy.

8. Children

Trayzy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, most requests are answered by using the choices in section 4 or by contacting Google. You can also contact us at the address in section 1 and we will help.

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Estonia this is the Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon), www.aki.ee.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game starts to process information differently, for example by adding analytics or in-app purchases, we will update this policy before the change takes effect and change the effective date above.
